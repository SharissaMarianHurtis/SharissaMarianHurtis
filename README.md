## About Me

I work at the intersection of **software engineering, systems, data, and problem-solving**, building applications, working with APIs and databases, and understanding how different components come together to make a system work reliably.

My experience spans **software development, backend systems, SQL, REST APIs, application support, debugging, testing, and technical troubleshooting**. I’m interested in the engineering behind an application — from its backend logic and data flow to the way different services and components interact.

My current technical focus includes **Java, Python, SQL, backend engineering, software systems, APIs, and modern development technologies**.

Beyond software development, I explore **Artificial Intelligence (AI), AI-assisted development, automation, and emerging applications of computing** through projects, academic work, technical research, and technology writing.

My academic interests also extend into **AI, synthetic biology, computational biology, genetics, and healthcare technology**. During my MCA, I explored areas including **CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, AI-assisted gene editing, protein structure prediction, and computational approaches to biological research**.

I also contribute to technology writing through published articles covering **Artificial Intelligence, software development, programming, developer tools, programming languages, and emerging technologies**.

During my MCA, I served as **Team Lead of TECHTITTANS** at **Smart India Hackathon 2023**, representing Rajagiri College of Social Sciences, where the team advanced to the **Grand Finale in the Software category**.
